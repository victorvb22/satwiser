# Step 3 — main model and event classification

Generated by `scripts/run_step3.py`. Everything fitted or tuned uses the calibration period only (2014-08-01 to 2020-01-01); the test period is 2020-01-01 to 2026-06-13, the end of the ESA manoeuvre record (orbits run to 2026-06-30; later detections cannot be scored).

- Autocorrelation of the raw mean-a residuals at a lag of 175 revolutions (the 12-day repeat cycle): **0.995** (lag 1: 0.132, lag 29: 0.897), computed on quadratically detrended quiet segments. Most of the step 2 "noise" is a deterministic function of the position on the ground track.
- Template amplitude (peak to peak): a 23.5 m, i 4.48 mdeg, e 28.0 ×1e-6.

## 2. Drag model

- Power law fitted on 233 quiet calibration segments: `-da/drev = exp(-15.35) · F81^2.64 · (F/F81)^1.04 · (1+Ap)^0.09` (m/rev).
- Median relative error of the segment decay rate: calibration 14 %, test 19 % (test includes the 2024 solar maximum, outside the calibration range of F10.7).
- An exponential law in F81 fitted the calibration equally well but overshot the 2024 decay by a factor of two; a daily-resolution fit was worse (single-day slopes are too noisy). Mean decay per year:

### False alarms on the test period

- 91 false alarms. 30 fall within two days of a geomagnetic storm (daily Ap ≥ 30): 33 % of false alarms, against 14 % of true detections and 11 % of test days. Storms raise the thermospheric density for a day or two, which the daily-index drag model only partly captures; storm-associated alarms are consistent with density-driven decay changes rather than manoeuvres.
- 56 false alarms have |Δa| ≤ 3 m (drag-model residuals); 35 have |Δa| > 3 m, of which 1 coincide(s) with a POD manoeuvre flag (probable manoeuvres missing from the ESA record). They are still counted as false alarms.

## 4. Classification of detected events

Classes: `station_keeping` (matched to an ESA station-keeping manoeuvre), `orbit_change` (matched to an inclination, multi-burn sequence or lowering manoeuvre), `unexplained` (no ESA manoeuvre within tolerance). The third class is the operational definition agreed at step 1: the ESA record has no anomaly class.

| label           |   calibration |   test |
|:----------------|--------------:|-------:|
| station_keeping |           135 |    289 |
| orbit_change    |           100 |    124 |
| unexplained     |            17 |     91 |

- Rule baseline (|Δi| > 0.17 mdeg or Δa < −1.56 m → orbit change; Δa > 1.56 m → station keeping; otherwise unexplained): macro F1 **0.762**, accuracy 0.827.
- Gradient-boosted trees trained on calibration detections. Feature set chosen by 3-fold time-ordered cross-validation inside the calibration period (macro F1: all 0.674, geometry 0.586): **all** (da, de, di, abs_da, abs_di, dv_est, stat_abs, alarm_lag, on_i_channel, f107_81d, ap, days_since_prev). Test macro F1 **0.721**, accuracy 0.800.
- `unexplained` examples: 17 in calibration, 91 in test. The learned model does not beat the rule on the test period: the calibration period (quiet Sun) holds few `unexplained` examples, while the test period is dominated by solar-maximum drag events.
