# Step 3 — main model and event classification

Generated by `scripts/run_step3.py`. Everything fitted or tuned uses the calibration period only (2014-08-01 to 2020-01-01); the test period is 2020-01-01 to 2026-06-13, the end of the ESA manoeuvre record (orbits run to 2026-06-30; later detections cannot be scored).

## 1. Ground-track repeat signature

- Autocorrelation of the raw mean-a residuals at a lag of 175 revolutions (the 12-day repeat cycle): **0.995** (lag 1: 0.132, lag 29: 0.897), computed on quadratically detrended quiet segments. Most of the step 2 "noise" is a deterministic function of the position on the ground track.
- Template amplitude (peak to peak): a 23.5 m, i 4.48 mdeg, e 28.0 ×1e-6.

The noise level used as reference is the median monthly noise over the whole mission (an unsupervised statistic, no labels). The 48 ESA manoeuvres before that date (orbit acquisition, satellite not yet on its reference ground track) are excluded from the evaluation of every detector below.

## 2. Drag model

- Power law fitted on 233 quiet calibration segments (0 with a non-negative slope are left out of the log fit): `-da/drev = exp(-15.32) · F81^2.64 · (F/F81)^1.20 · (1+Ap)^0.06` (m/rev), where F81 is the trailing 81-day mean of F10.7.
- Median relative error of the segment decay rate: calibration 13 %, test 15 % (test includes the 2024 solar maximum, outside the calibration range of F10.7).
- Alternative: an exponential law in F81 fitted on the same segments predicts 1.83 times the observed mean 2024 decay, against 0.95 for the power law, which is why the power law is kept (2024 lies beyond the calibration range of F10.7). Mean decay per year:

## 3. Detector comparison

- Main detector: two-sided CUSUM on the template-corrected semi-major axis and inclination, innovations clipped at 6σ. Two variants for the semi-major axis: drag model + trailing mean, or trailing line without drag model. Selected by calibration F1 over 120 settings: **full**, memory 20, κ = 2, h = 6, scale window 200.
- The windowed baseline is recalibrated on the template-corrected series for a fair comparison. All detectors share the evaluation protocol of step 2 (one-revolution tolerance, ESA manoeuvres as truth) and the same observability window (12 revolutions on each side), so they are scored on the same manoeuvres.
- Delays are orbit time from the manoeuvre start to the alarm, without the publication latency of the precise orbits (about three weeks). The CUSUM decides from past revolutions only (its drag correction uses same-day F10.7 and Ap and the trailing 81-day F10.7 mean, a look-ahead of at most one day on these covariates); the baselines normalise with a centred window of about 15 days, so their delays are not causal and only indicative.

### False alarms on the test period

- 76 false alarms. 27 fall within two days of a geomagnetic storm (daily Ap ≥ 30): 36 % of false alarms, against 14 % of true detections and 11 % of test days. Storms raise the thermospheric density for a day or two, which the daily-index drag model only partly captures; storm-associated alarms are consistent with density-driven decay changes rather than manoeuvres.
- 43 false alarms have |Δa| ≤ 3 m (drag-model residuals); 33 have |Δa| > 3 m, of which 0 coincide(s) with a POD manoeuvre flag (probable manoeuvres missing from the ESA record). They are still counted as false alarms.

## 4. Classification of detected events

Classes: `station_keeping` (matched to an ESA station-keeping manoeuvre), `orbit_change` (matched to an inclination, multi-burn sequence or lowering manoeuvre), `unexplained` (no ESA manoeuvre within tolerance). The third class is the operational definition agreed at step 1: the ESA record has no anomaly class.

| label           |   calibration |   test |
|:----------------|--------------:|-------:|
| station_keeping |           136 |    288 |
| orbit_change    |           100 |    124 |
| unexplained     |            15 |     76 |

- Rule baseline (|Δi| > 0.17 mdeg or Δa < −1.56 m → orbit change; Δa > 1.56 m → station keeping; otherwise unexplained): macro F1 **0.752**, accuracy 0.830.
- Gradient-boosted trees trained on calibration detections. Feature set chosen by 3-fold time-ordered cross-validation inside the calibration period (macro F1: all 0.615, geometry 0.578): **all** (da, de, di, abs_da, abs_di, dv_est, stat_abs, alarm_lag, on_i_channel, f107_81d, ap, days_since_prev). Test macro F1 **0.757**, accuracy 0.822.
- `unexplained` examples: 15 in calibration, 76 in test. The learned model beats the rule on the test period.
- Disclosure: the choice of which classifier the app displays was made after looking at these test scores, so it is not an out-of-sample model selection.
